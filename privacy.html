Privacy Policy

Kwentong Barbero — effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains what data is collected when you use Kwentong Barbero, why it is collected, and how long it is kept.

Who runs this service

Kwentong Barbero is operated by Arvin Cudanin. Contact: barberoscutmedia@gmail.com.

What we collect

Publishing credentials

To publish to a social platform, the publisher stores the access and refresh tokens issued by that platform, together with the account identifiers it needs to post (for example a TikTok open_id, a Facebook Page id, an Instagram professional account id). These are stored locally in a configuration file on the machine running the publisher, in a file that is excluded from version control. They are never transmitted to us, and we cannot read them.

Data held by the platforms

When you publish, the platform you publish to receives the video file, the caption, the tags and the disclosure settings you chose. Those platforms process that data under their own privacy policies, which govern what happens to it after publication. Once content is published, its handling is governed by those policies, not by this one.

Local publishing history

The publisher keeps a local log of what it has published — the story id, the platform, a hash of the video file and the publication date — so that a file is not published twice by mistake. This log stays on your machine.

Service logs

The publishing tool writes a local log file with error messages and request diagnostics. Access tokens and secrets are redacted before anything is written. These logs stay on your machine and are not sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or store your browsing behaviour, your location, your contacts, your device identifiers, or the contents of the videos you publish. We do not operate analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Who we share it with

Nothing. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. The only entities that receive your content are the social platforms you explicitly choose to publish to, at the moment you publish.

Data retention

Publishing credentials stay in the local configuration file until you remove them or revoke access on the platform's side. You can revoke a platform's access at any time from that platform's own account settings; revoking it there also invalidates the stored token. Local logs and the publishing history remain on your machine until you delete them.

Your rights

Because the data described above stays on your own machine, you can inspect, edit or delete it yourself at any time. For anything held by a social platform — a published video, a comment, a follower, a profile — use that platform's own access, download and deletion tools, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.

Security

Credentials are kept in a local configuration file that is excluded from version control, and secrets are redacted from log output. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your machine protected with an account password and full-disk encryption.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The version published on this page is the version that applies.
