Data Deletion Instructions

Kwentong Barbero — effective 3 November 2026

This page explains how to request the deletion of your data, and how to delete it yourself. It applies to data held by Kwentong Barbero.

What Kwentong Barbero stores

Kwentong Barbero is a local publishing tool. It runs on your own machine, and the data it keeps stays on that machine. We do not operate a server that holds your account, and we cannot see your data.

The data that exists locally is:

  • Publishing credentials. Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, plus the account identifiers needed to post (a TikTok open_id, a Facebook Page id, an Instagram professional account id). These live in a local configuration file that is excluded from version control.
  • Publishing history. A local log recording the story id, the platform, a hash of the video file and the publication date, so a video is not published twice by mistake.
  • Service logs. Local log files containing error messages and request diagnostics. Access tokens and secrets are redacted before anything is written.

For a fuller description, see the Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data yourself

The quickest way to delete everything is to delete the local files. Both paths below are relative to the project directory:

  • Delete the .env file to remove every stored credential. This immediately stops the publisher from being able to post on your behalf.
  • Delete the output/publish_log.json file to remove the publishing history.
  • Delete the logs/ directory to remove the service logs.

You can also revoke each platform's access independently. Removing a platform's permission from that platform's own app settings invalidates the stored token for that platform, whether or not the local file is deleted.

Requesting deletion from us

If you would like the local data removed on your behalf, email barberoscutmedia@gmail.com from the address associated with the account and tell us which platform's data you want removed. We will confirm once it has been done.

Data held by the social platforms

Content you publish is held by the platform you publish to — a published video, its comments, likes, followers and profile are governed by that platform's own privacy policy and deletion tools. Kwentong Barbero does not mirror, copy or retain that data, so we cannot delete it for you. Use the platform's own access, download and deletion tools, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.
