Kwentong Barbero

Philippine folklore, told in short vertical video.

Kwentong Barbero publishes short vertical videos about Philippine folklore, urban legends and the stories told during barber visits — the ones about the tenant upstairs, the komiks in the neighbourhood, the thing in the mall parking lot.

The narration, scripts and visuals are AI-generated, and every video carries an AI-generated-content disclosure. The stories are folklore and rumour, told as such and debunked in the video itself. Nothing here is journalism or documentary.
